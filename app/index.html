    How this guide was put together

    • Official names, descriptions and grades are shown as PlayStation publishes them, from the PS5 list as Exophase and PSNProfiles listed it (reported by Push Square, 18 November 2026). One name carries asterisks in the official list: “T**s Ass Drug Money”.
    • Hidden trophies (28 of 55): which trophies PlayStation hides until earned follows the published list — every story trophy is hidden, plus the secret-event trophies. The split is 27 shown, 28 hidden.
    • Missable (1 of 55): only Positive Mental Attitude is flagged missable, as GTABase and PowerPyx both report — Lucia's Chapter 1 balcony meditation disappears after Chapter 1. Everything else stays available after the credits, including the side-story finales.
    • Guides are written in the author's own words for this guide. They draw on the sources above and cite them; they never copy another guide's text. Where no source had confirmed a method yet (five hidden trophies at launch), the guide says so plainly instead of guessing.
    • Counts (42 Shooting Range challenges, 14 drone race locations, 18 meditation spots, 9 activity types, 52 areas, 9 gangs, 8 garages, 500 Criminal Profile actions) come from the official list as Exophase and PSNProfiles listed it, corroborated where noted by Output Lag.
